## 01 — Brand Overview

**52 TOOLS** is a modern collection of powerful, precise, and reliable tools for creators, developers, and problem solvers.

The identity is built to feel:

- **Modern**
- **Precise**
- **Technical**
- **Minimal**
- **Intelligent**
- **Reliable**
- **Utility-focused**

### Brand Principles

1. **Precision** — Every element is deliberate and exact.
2. **Utility** — Design exists to serve a clear purpose.
3. **Intelligence** — The system communicates technical capability.
4. **Speed** — The experience feels efficient and focused.
5. **Simplicity** — Unnecessary visual noise is removed.
6. **Technology** — A modern digital design language, without becoming overly futuristic.

### Visual Philosophy

The 52 Tools identity is built entirely in **black and white**, expressed through **sharp geometry**, **confident typography**, and **generous whitespace**. Nothing is decorative — every mark, every space, and every weight exists because it serves the system. The brand should read as a **precision instrument**, not a generic technology template.

## 02 — Logo

![52 Tools Logo](./assets/52-tools-logo.png)

The 52 Tools identity is composed of two core elements:

- **The 52 Monogram** — a custom, geometrically constructed symbol built from the numerals "5" and "2," with a circular negative space at its core evoking a wrench head / tool socket.
- **The `52 TOOLS` Wordmark** — a futuristic, technical, evenly spaced logotype set in all caps.

### Relationship Between Monogram and Wordmark

The monogram acts as the primary brand mark and can stand alone. The wordmark reinforces the name in full applications and is always set below the monogram, centered, at a fixed proportional distance.

> **Important:** The monogram geometry is custom-drawn and must never be recreated, redrawn, modified, stretched, or substituted using a standard font or third-party glyph. It is a fixed asset, not a typographic character.

## 03 — Logo Variations

### Primary Lockup

- **Description:** Black monogram + black `52 TOOLS` wordmark, stacked vertically.
- **Background usage:** Light backgrounds only (Soft White `#FAFAFA` or White `#FFFFFF`).
- **Recommended use:** Default lockup for light-mode contexts — documents, light UI, packaging, print.

### Reverse Lockup

- **Description:** White monogram + white `52 TOOLS` wordmark, stacked vertically.
- **Background usage:** Dark backgrounds only (Obsidian Black `#0B0B0C`, Graphite `#1C1C1E`, or Pure Black `#000000`).
- **Recommended use:** Dark-mode interfaces, dark presentations, video, dark packaging.

### Monogram

- **Description:** The 52 symbol on its own, without the wordmark.
- **Background usage:** Light backgrounds.
- **Recommended use:** Small-format placements, app icons, social avatars, favicons, and any space where the full lockup would compromise legibility.

### Reverse Monogram

- **Description:** White 52 symbol, without the wordmark.
- **Background usage:** Dark backgrounds.
- **Recommended use:** Dark-mode icons, dark avatars, dark small-format placements.

| Version | Color | Background | Primary Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary Lockup | Black | Light | Default, full identity |
| Reverse Lockup | White | Dark | Dark-mode, full identity |
| Monogram | Black | Light | Small formats, icons |
| Reverse Monogram | White | Dark | Small formats, dark icons |

## 04 — Logo Clear Space

The logo must always be surrounded by a **protected area of empty space** to preserve its visual integrity and impact.

### Clear-Space Rule

- The minimum clear space on all four sides is equal to **X**, where **X = 1/4 of the monogram width**.
- This applies uniformly to the top, bottom, left, and right of the full lockup or monogram.

### Minimum Protected Area

The protected area scales proportionally with the logo. As the logo grows or shrinks, the clear space (`X`) scales with it — it is always relative, never a fixed pixel value.

### What May Not Enter the Clear-Space Area

- Text
- Icons
- Borders
- Images
- Decorative elements
- Other logos or brand marks

> The clear-space zone is non-negotiable. If a layout cannot accommodate it, the logo size should be reduced (respecting minimum size rules) rather than the clear space compromised.

## 05 — Logo Construction

The 52 monogram is built on a **strict geometric grid system**, ensuring mathematical consistency across every use of the mark.

### Construction Principles

- **Grid-based construction** — every stroke, angle, and terminal aligns to an underlying modular grid.
- **Geometric relationships** — the "5" and "2" forms share consistent stroke widths, angles, and proportions.
- **Circular central negative space** — a perfect circle sits at the optical center, referencing a wrench socket / tool head and unifying the two numerals into a single mark.
- **Horizontal alignment** — key terminals and stroke edges align along shared horizontal guides.
- **Vertical alignment** — the vertical axis of the "5" and "2" are balanced around a common centerline.
- **Consistent proportions** — stroke weight remains uniform throughout the mark.
- **Repeated geometric logic** — angles and curves used in one part of the mark are echoed elsewhere, reinforcing a single unified system rather than two unrelated characters.

This construction system exists to ensure the monogram can be reproduced with precision — it must never be freehanded, approximated, or redrawn from reference.

## 06 — Logo Proportions

The relationship between all elements of the lockup is **fixed** and must be preserved in every application.

### Fixed Relationships

- **Monogram height** to **monogram width** — fixed ratio, never distorted.
- **Wordmark width** — proportional to monogram width.
- **Wordmark letter spacing** — fixed tracking value, never condensed or expanded.
- **Vertical relationship** — fixed distance between the base of the monogram and the top of the wordmark, expressed as a proportion of the monogram height.

> **The logo proportions must never be altered.** Scaling must always be uniform (locked aspect ratio) — never stretched, compressed, or skewed on a single axis.

### Full Lockup Specification

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| Monogram width | `B` (base unit) |
| Monogram height | `4A` (4 stacked proportional units) |
| Wordmark width | Matches monogram width (`B`) |
| Vertical gap (monogram → wordmark) | Fixed proportional unit (`X`) |
| Scaling | Uniform only — lock aspect ratio |

## 07 — Minimum Size

To preserve legibility and the integrity of the geometric construction, the logo must never be scaled below its defined minimum sizes.

### Full Logo

- **Minimum digital width:** `120px`

### Monogram

- **Minimum digital width:** `32px`

### Usage Guidance

- Use the **monogram-only** version whenever space constraints would force the full lockup below its minimum width — favicons, app icons, small avatars, compact UI elements.
- The full lockup should never be reduced to a size where the `52 TOOLS` wordmark becomes difficult to read. If legibility is in question, switch to the monogram rather than shrinking further.

## 09 — Typography

The type system is built on three typefaces, each with a distinct functional role.

### Primary Typeface — Space Grotesk

Use for:

- Headings
- Brand statements
- Navigation
- Large labels
- Key information

```
# 52 TOOLS
## PRECISION ENGINEERED
```

### Secondary Typeface — Inter

Use for:

- Body copy
- Descriptions
- Documentation
- Supporting information

```
52 Tools is a modern collection of powerful, precise,
and reliable tools for creators, developers, and problem solvers.
```

### Monospace Typeface — IBM Plex Mono

Use for:

- HEX values
- Measurements
- Technical specifications
- Numbers
- Code-related information

```
#0B0B0C   120px   32px   4A
```

## 11 — Logo Do & Don't

### Do Not:

- ❌ Stretch the logo
- ❌ Compress the logo
- ❌ Rotate the logo
- ❌ Add drop shadows
- ❌ Add gradients
- ❌ Add outlines
- ❌ Change the logo colors
- ❌ Modify the monogram geometry
- ❌ Change wordmark spacing
- ❌ Place the logo on busy backgrounds
- ❌ Recreate the logo using another typeface

> If a use case is not explicitly covered here, default to the most conservative, unmodified application of the logo — never improvise a variation.

## 12 — Favicon

The favicon system uses the **52 monogram only**. Do not include the `52 TOOLS` wordmark in favicon or app-icon contexts.

### Recommended Sizes

- `16 × 16`
- `32 × 32`
- `48 × 48`
- `64 × 64`
- `128 × 128`

At extremely small sizes, the monogram's circular negative space and core geometry must remain clearly recognizable. If clarity is compromised at a given size, simplify supporting details before altering the core geometry.
